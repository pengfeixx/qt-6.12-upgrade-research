## 6. 示例 manifest 关键字段（linuxdeepin/deepin-editor/linglong.yaml）

```yaml
base: org.deepin.base/25.2.2
runtime: org.deepin.runtime.webengine/6.7.0   # Qt/DTK 来自运行时 layer
build: |
  cmake -B build ... -DCMAKE_PREFIX_PATH=/runtime   # 构建期从 /runtime 解析 Qt6/DTK6
buildext:
  apt:
    build_depends: [cmake, libchardet-dev, libicu-dev, libkf6codecs-dev, ...]
```

含义：玲珑应用的构建期与运行期 Qt 均由 runtime layer 提供，与系统 deb 的 qt6-base-dev 完全解耦；buildext.apt 只装辅助依赖。
